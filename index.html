Ocho SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Ocho SMS (package com.ochosms.app) checks text messages for scam warning signs and alerts you. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

Summary

  • Scam detection runs on your phone.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your messages, contacts or phone number.
  • We run no servers for this app and have no user accounts.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Data the app accesses

  • SMS and MMS messages (receive, read, send): to check incoming messages for scam signs, show your message history inside the app, and, if you choose Ocho SMS as your default SMS app, to send and receive messages. Messages are stored only on your device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Contacts: read on your device to lower the risk score of messages from people you know and to show names. Contacts are never copied or uploaded.
  • Notifications: to show scam warnings.
  • Your block and trusted lists and settings: stored only on your device.

Internet use

The only network feature is an optional link check with Google Safe Browsing. It is off unless you enter your own Google API key in Settings. If you turn it on, the web addresses (URLs) found in a message are sent to Google to check whether they are dangerous. The message text, the sender and your contacts are not sent. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups

If you export a backup, it is saved to a file you choose, encrypted with a passphrase you set. We cannot read it and cannot recover a lost passphrase. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. The app's developer has no access to your messages or contacts.

Retention and deletion

All data stays on your phone. You can delete messages inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

Ocho SMS is not directed at children under 13.
